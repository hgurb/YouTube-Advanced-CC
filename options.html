Subtitle size

Choose a fixed step or your own list of subtitle sizes for scrolling.

10–499%, below the maximum. Invalid values reset to 10% when you leave the field or save.

11–500%, above the minimum. Invalid values reset to 500% when you leave the field or save.

1–100 percentage points per scroll.

Separate whole numbers with commas, without % signs. When you leave this field or save, values below 10 become 10 and values above 500 become 500. The list is sorted and duplicates removed. Keep at least 2 different sizes (up to 100 entries). This list sets its own minimum and maximum.

Preferred languages

Add a language name or keyword as it appears in your YouTube menu, such as Spanish, Español, or Japanese. Matching ignores capitalization and accents and includes variants such as “English (auto-generated)”. English/İngilizce and Turkish/Türkçe are recognized as pairs.

Up to 20 entries, 60 characters each. Use ↑ and ↓ to set the menu order after Off. If a language matches several keywords, the first match wins. Variants and unmatched languages keep YouTube’s order within their groups. Remove all entries to keep the original order. Applies when using the Settings cog shortcut.

Click Save to apply your settings.

Import & export

Export your current settings as text to copy to another browser. Import loads settings into the editor for review. Click Save to apply them, or Discard to keep your saved settings. Includes both sizing modes, the cog shortcut, and preferred languages in order. Appearance stays local.
